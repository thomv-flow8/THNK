Privacy

Short version: this website has no accounts, no newsletter, no advertising and no analytics. Nothing you do here is tracked, and no cookies are set by THNK.

What happens when you visit

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details in its server logs so it can deliver the pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Third parties loaded on this site

  • Fonts and artwork are served from this website itself, so nothing about them reaches a third party.
  • Spotify — the player on the home page comes from Spotify and may place cookies once it loads. See the Spotify Privacy Policy.

Email

If you email the booking address, THNK receives whatever you put in that message: your name, your email address and anything else you choose to share. It is used to answer you and to arrange bookings, and it is not shared with anyone else.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Send that request to the booking address and it will be handled within 30 days. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

If this site ever adds analytics, a newsletter or a form, this page will be updated before that goes live.
